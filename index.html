About

Glitches & Stitches makes bass music out of broken things: corrupted samples, dropped frames, and sounds that weren't supposed to work, cut apart and sewn back together into something you can dance to.

Every live set is built from scratch, with visuals glitched in real time. The sample packs and presets in the shop are the actual sounds from those sets.
