# Store discovery

The store page (`/shop`) is a search over the real catalog in D1, with filters, sorting and pages answered by the server, plus curated rows ranked for the visitor. Everything a product claims comes from its own data; nothing (reviews, sales, compatibility) is invented to fill a label. The owner's choices behind it: [decisions](decisions.md) (2026-10-09, "Store recommendations stay on the visitor's device").

## Metadata

- **Vocabulary:** `server/discovery/vocabulary.js` is the one list of structured fields (facets) and allowed values: genre, style, mood, energy, made for (`use`), contents, file format, DAW, plugin, key, system (`os`), level (`skill`), plus BPM ranges and specs (sample rate, bit depth). Synonyms fold creators' words into one value (`dnb` → Drum & Bass, `one-shot` → One-shots); unknown words are refused, never stored. `data/vocabulary.js` is its browser copy, generated by `scripts/build.js` (a unit test keeps them equal).
- **Per kind:** `SCHEMAS` says which facets mean something for each kind of product (the keys of `data/product-types.js`): BPM for sample packs, stems and music but not visuals; plugins for presets and VJ loops but not sample packs. A filter only appears where it applies; a value for a field that doesn't apply is refused.
- **Storage:** `product_facets` (one row per product, facet, value; migration `0012`), plus `bpm_min`/`bpm_max`, `released_at` and `search_text` on `products`. Catalog products get them from `data/products.js` (`facets`, `specs`, `bpm`, `released`) through `db/catalog.sql`; without its own genre a catalog product takes its project's `genres`. Studio products get them from the creator's "Store details" (`js/studio-details.js`), checked again on the server (`server/creators/products.js`). Derived scores never live with the product's data.
- **Suggestions:** the Studio shows values a product's own title, description and tags mention (`suggestFacets`); the creator ticks them or not. Nothing is applied automatically.

## Search API

`GET /api/store/search` (public, cached 60 s): `q`, `category`, any facet (`genre=house,techno`), `creator` (project ids), `price_min`/`price_max` (dollars), `free=1|0`, `sale=1`, `new=1` (released or added in the last 30 days), `bpm_min`/`bpm_max`, `sort` (relevance, bestselling, newest, price-asc, price-desc, discount, name), `page`, `per` (up to 48).

- **Semantics:** OR within a group, AND between groups. A tempo filter needs a stated tempo (products without one drop out while it's set); every other missing detail only matters when that detail is filtered. Malformed input is a 400 (`invalid_filter`); a well-formed value the vocabulary doesn't know (an old link) is dropped and listed in `ignored`.
- **Prices:** every statement reads products through one CTE that applies running sales exactly like `server/promotions` `salePrice` (the minimum charge included), so the price filtered, sorted and shown is the price checkout charges. "On sale" and "discount" are only true while a sale runs.
- **Counts:** each facet group is counted with every other filter applied but not itself (so its other values stay reachable); categories, creators, price and BPM ranges and the toggles likewise. One `db.batch`; lists go in as one JSON parameter (`json_each`), far below D1's parameter limit.
- **Best-selling:** active purchase entitlements in the last 90 days (refunds revoke them; carts and wishlists never count). Cards show only a floor bucket ("5+ bought recently", 10+, 25+...), never an exact number, and nothing under 5.
- **Search:** words match `search_text` (title, description, tags, facet labels, maker); a title hit ranks higher. `LIKE` over a few thousand rows is fast enough today (see Limits).
- `GET /api/store/suggest?q=` (autocomplete: matching facet values with counts, creators, product titles), `GET /api/store/similar/:id` (non-personal look-alikes for a product page).
- Ownership is never in these answers: cards ask `/api/products/state` (the one place that decides "owned"), so a product you own never shows as unowned.

## Recommendations

`POST /api/store/recommendations` (never cached). The browser sends a summary of its own history (`js/taste.js` `profile()`); the server adds what the account holds (confirmed purchases; the wishlist while personal picks are on), then:

1. **Eligibility first, in SQL:** visible, not paused, within the visitor's stated limits (`constraints`: the same parameters as search, e.g. a price ceiling or `daw=ableton-live`), and not owned, dismissed or in the cart. Up to 400 candidates: most sold (60%), newest (20%) and a random slice (20%), so new products get a chance in a large catalog.
2. **Score** (`server/discovery/rank.js`, weights in `server/discovery/weights.js`): the weighted mean of components that are each 0..1, so raw counts, prices and percentages are never added together:

| Component | Weight | How it is normalized |
| --- | --- | --- |
| preference | 0.30 | Per taste facet (genre, style, mood, use, energy, contents, kind) the best match among the product's values, each facet weighted; a facet the product doesn't state counts 0.3 (neutral), not 0 |
| search | 0.20 | Share of the recent search words found (title hit 1, elsewhere 0.5) |
| behavior | 0.18 | Similarity to the closest product engaged with, times how much that engagement says |
| price | 0.07 | 1 − (log-price distance from the middle of explored prices) / 2; 0 without price history |
| quality | 0.05 | 1 for a verified creator, 0.5 otherwise (reviews would join here) |
| popularity | 0.08 | log(1 + 3·purchases + 2·saves + 0.1·views), divided by the catalog's highest |
| freshness | 0.05 | Linear from 1 (today) to 0 (60 days) |
| discovery | 0.07 | 0.5 outside the visitor's usual kinds and genres + 0.3 if seldom viewed + 0.2 × a daily rotation per device |

   Then: × (1 − 0.12 per time shown and passed over, at most −50%), × (1 − 0.5 × likeness to a dismissed product), × 0.75 for something already opened, carted or saved.
3. **Evidence weights** (`EVIDENCE`): save or explicit choice 1, cart 0.7, preview 0.5, owned 0.4, opened from a list 0.25, product page view 0.15, filter 0.3, dismissed −0.6. Repeats count less each time. A view is weak evidence; a purchase shapes taste but the product itself is never offered again.
4. **Diversify and build sections:** at most 2 products per creator (project) in a section, each further one from the same creator or genre worth less; each product at most once across sections; a section with fewer than 2 products is left out. Sections: Recommended for you (only with some history), Popular in your genres, Similar to your favorites (with saved, carted or owned products), Bestselling (3+ confirmed purchases in 90 days), Deals matching your interests / On sale now, Recently added, Free and worth trying, Discover something different.

The current search and filters always win: while the store is narrowed, the sections step aside and results are ordered only by the chosen sort. Recommendation generation takes tens of milliseconds locally on a 3,000-product catalog (`test/api/discovery.suite.js` prints the timings).

## History, privacy and control

- **On device only:** `js/taste.js` keeps one event log in `localStorage` (`gs:taste:v2`) with one schema `{ t, id?, facet?, value?, q?, at }` for view, click, preview, cart, wishlist/unwishlist, dismiss, search, filter and impression. Policy: a view of the same product within 30 minutes or an impression on the same day isn't counted again; events older than 90 days and beyond 400 are dropped; purchases are never inferred (the server knows confirmed ones).
- **Sent, not kept:** the summary travels with each recommendation request and is used for that answer only: not stored, logged or linked to the account. "Forget my history" clears it; "Turn off personal picks" clears it and stops recording (everyone then sees the same rows). /privacy says so.
- **Wishlists** (`server/wishlist/`, `wishlist_items`): signed-in only, visible products only, up to 500; each person reads and changes only their own; deleted with the account and part of the data export. Others see only aggregate counts (popularity).

## Pages

- `/shop`: `js/store-browse.js` (state in the address, results, chips, pages, the filter drawer on phones; offline it shows `data/products.js`), `js/store-filters.js` (panel and chip templates), `js/store-suggest.js` (combobox), `js/store-sections.js` (rows, tune picks, not interested, your wishlist, coming soon), `js/wishlist.js` (hearts), `css/store.css`.
- Product pages: every stated detail in "Details", a wishlist button, and "You might also like" (`js/similar.js`).
- Other pages' grids (`js/shop.js`) stay simple: featured or a project's products, each opening its own page.

## Limits and next steps

- **No reviews or ratings yet:** there is no review data, so there is no rating filter, sort or badge; the quality component uses verified creators only. Reviews tied to confirmed purchases would slot into `quality` (issue #96).
- **Search is `LIKE` over `search_text`:** fine for thousands of products; move to D1's FTS5 if the catalog or latency grows (issue #97).
- **Signals are cached 60 s per Worker instance;** best-selling counts are read live.
- **Placeholder catalog:** AutoStacc's own products are drafts (not in the store database in production), so the live store shows Studio products plus a "Coming soon" row from `data/products.js`.
