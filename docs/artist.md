# Glitches & Stitches

Only confirmed facts go here. Don't invent anything; ask the owner.

## Confirmed

- Electronic/EDM artist project named "Glitches & Stitches". The owner confirmed this exact spelling on 2026-10-07 (never "Glitches and Stitches", "Stiches" or "AutoStacc"; autostacc.com is only the domain).
- The project is a method: unfinished songs are raw material; pieces are extracted, stitched into new tracks, glitched, and the results reused again ("remix²"), so the catalog becomes interconnected. Full description in [identity](identity.md).
- Released music: **HAIL**, a single released 2025-10-15 (2:27, distributed through DistroKid, UPC 199746791737, ISRC QT3FF2517426). Verified on [Deezer](https://www.deezer.com/track/3605198432), [Apple Music](https://music.apple.com/us/album/hail/1847091235?i=1847091237) and [YouTube Music](https://music.youtube.com/watch?v=EzVo6Mc8Yt0) (link from the owner; YouTube's auto-generated channel for it is named "Release - Topic", and YouTube lists it in 123 countries, not worldwide). The owner writes the title as HAIL; stores show "Hail". Apple Music lists the artist as "Glitches & Stiches" (missing a t); Deezer has it right. Deezer album page: https://www.deezer.com/album/838974702 (label shown as "5727920 Records DK", DistroKid's default; genres Electro, Dance). Cover art: logs burning in a bright orange fire on black, stored in `img/releases/`. Spotify and SoundCloud links not found yet: ask the owner.
- Sound: EDM/electronic, deliberately not one narrow subgenre (glitching, chopping, resampling, heavy sound design, chaotic parts against melodic ones). Experimental, never random.

- Booking and press contact: support@autostacc.com, the same address as AutoStacc support (owner, 2026-10-08).
