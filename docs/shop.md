# Shop

Products are defined in `data/products.js` (fields documented at its top) with their kinds in `data/product-types.js` (music, stems, samples, sample-pack, presets, visuals, vj-loops, other: each sets its label, quantity limit, preview kind, track colour and licence). Each names the project that created it (`project`) and may list the Pacs it delivers (`pacs`); the product page shows "Created by" the project, "Sold by" AutoStacc and its Pacs. Model: [architecture](architecture.md). They are sold through the site's own store: cart, Stripe Checkout, accounts, library and private downloads. How that works: [store](store.md); switching it on: [store-setup](store-setup.md).

Every product is still a placeholder (tracked in GitHub issues), so every one is `draft: true`: shown in the shop as "Coming soon", its page (`shop/<id>.html`, generated by `node scripts/build.js`, at `/shop/<id>`) `noindex`, out of the sitemap, no product structured data, and not buyable (the store treats drafts as unpublished). Remove `draft` once the product, price and files are real.
