# Community

> **OPEN (owner, 2026-10-21).** The read side, posting and moderation (#135, #136, #137, copyright #167–#169) are live: `community.forum` is switched on in `wrangler.toml` (`FEATURES_ON`, production and preview). The owner opened it with some launch gates still open ([community safety](community-safety.md)). Later steps (below) are planned only, including each creator's Community page, where their news posts will move (#174).

"Like Reddit, but less of a do-what-you-want" (owner): a place to ask questions and get answers, share and have fun, and grow communities without needing a creator, inside rules AutoStacc sets.

**Scope (owner):** music and sound (making, sharing and talking about it, sound design, gear and software, the creators and projects on AutoStacc, challenges) and ideas and thoughts about products and AutoStacc. It isn't a general social site: spaces and threads outside that are off-topic and can be removed, and chat goes in a space's own off-topic channel. The aim is a space that stays welcoming to people coming in, not hostile to them.

**Who is accountable (owner):** each space's owner and team moderate it. AutoStacc does only what the law requires of the site and what can be done automatically or simply, which also keeps the legal duties as few as possible ([community safety](community-safety.md)).

## What the owner chose

| Question | Choice |
|---|---|
| Spaces | AutoStacc's own, an Official space each project can turn on, and **communities any verified account can start** |
| Structure | Each space has **channels** its owners set up |
| Post types | Discussion, **Question**, **Update**, **Poll**, **Challenge** |
| Q&A | A Question post type in spaces **and** a central Q&A page gathering questions from every space by tag |
| Answers | The asker (or a moderator) accepts one; others get **Helpful** votes (up only) |
| Threads | **Upvotes** (no downvotes) drive Hot and Top sorting |
| Fun | Challenges, Show your Stacc, games and events, badges and streaks |
| Polls and challenges | Hosted by creators (in their project space) and moderators (in a community) |
| Communities about a creator | Allowed, labelled **Unofficial**; only the creator's own space is **Official** |
| Rules | AutoStacc **base rules** everywhere, never loosened; each space may add **stricter** rules |
| Posts | Text with simple formatting and links. Challenge entries may also be audio (below) |
| Visibility | Public and indexed. People appear by display name only |
| Age | **16+** to post or connect, self-declared, no verification system |
| News | Each creator's community space is its **Community** page, and that is where posts, updates and announcements (events' included) belong (owner, 2026-10-21, #174). Until the community opens, news is hidden: no News editor, no news pages, `/<creator>/<project>/news[/<slug>]` answer a 301 to the project's home, and the `project_news` rows wait in D1 to become the creator's community posts at the same addresses |
| Outside communities | None; everything is on autostacc.com |

## Look and words (owner, 2026-10-20)

Clean but charming, in the app's own theme (a friendlier DAW: [autostacc](autostacc.md#visual-identity-placeholder-logo)):
- **Words:** a thread is a **take** ("New take"); upvote is **Boost**; sorting is **Hot / Fresh / Top**; an accepted answer is the **Best answer**. Reply and Helpful stay plain. Plain words stay in tooltips and screen-reader labels. This is the community's own vocabulary (owner); the rest of the platform keeps its plain voice. Code and addresses keep "thread".
- **Lists** of takes are **Reddit-like rows** (owner, 2026-10-27, #230): one list, each row with its channel's track colour on the edge, Boost's count on the left, the title and one small line (space, kind, channel, who, when, replies); the title's link covers the row.
- **Boost** is a small **level meter**: pressing it makes the meter jump with a quick bounce and settle, the count beside it.
- **Beside a name**, until profile pictures exist: a **tiny waveform** drawn from the name (the same every time for a name), so people are easy to tell apart.
- **Writing** opens **inline** (a New take button on spaces and channels) with **Write / Preview** tabs (the preview is the server's own formatting), a few formatting buttons (bold, italic, link, list, quote) and drafts kept in the browser. **Replying** (owner, 2026-10-27, #230) is a one-line "Add a reply…" box under the take, above the replies, that grows on tap into the text box with Cancel and Reply; **Aa** shows the formatting buttons and preview, and Post as shows only for a team. Reply under a post opens the same box there, ready to type.
- **A post's actions** (owner, 2026-10-27, #230; `js/community-menu.js`, through the kit's `row(post)` and `menu(post)`): a small row with what stays in view (Boost on the take, Reply as a text link, Helpful and Best answer on questions) and a **⋯ menu** at its end with Share link, Save, Follow (on the take), Report, Block author, Edit and Delete (your own) and the team's tools under a Team label. The space's manage page stays where a team works through what's waiting.
- **Front page** (`/community`): a compact banner, a row of **spaces as cards** (the shared card, `js/card.js`: name, an Official / Unofficial corner tag, a small activity meter), then **Hot takes** from everywhere, and a box of **unanswered questions** for newcomers. Replies read as a conversation, without a frame each.
- **Charm**, all short and off for reduced motion: the boost bounce, new posts **stacking in**, friendly **empty-state** lines with a tiny illustration, and a soft **glow** when a best answer is picked.
- **Replies are fully nested** (any depth). On phones the indent stops growing after a few levels and deeper replies fold behind "more replies".

## Features (owner, 2026-10-20)

Each is its own feature with its own switch (see "Built as independent features" below):
- **In posts:** music embeds (SoundCloud, YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music links become players, rebuilt from the link like creator pages, never pasted code), **store item cards** (a link to an AutoStacc product, Pac or project shows as a card with picture and price from the store), and **code / settings blocks** kept exactly as typed.
- **Keeping up:** follow a take (your own and ones you reply to automatically), join a space (its takes come first on your front page), save for later (a private Saved list). No unread markers.
- **Notifications:** **@mentions** (blocked people can't mention you; mention alerts can be switched off) and an **on-site inbox** (a bell in the top bar: replies, mentions, best-answer picks), beside the configurable emails.
- **Finding:** community **search** (by words, filtered by space, channel and kind), **tags** (AutoStacc's starter list from the store vocabulary, plus a few each space's team adds; people pick from those), and **similar takes** shown while typing a new question's title.
- **Names:** a **mini profile card** on hover or tap (name, waveform, member since, Best answers, team roles), with **Block** and **Report**.
- **Team tools:** channels and rules, **pinned and featured** takes, a **welcome message** on a first visit or post, and the space's **look** (accent colour, banner from presets).
- **Store links:** **product takes** (a take per product, linked from its page), an opt-in **buyer badge** ("Owns this", since it reveals a purchase), and **release takes** opened automatically with an Update or release.
- **Self-promotion:** linking your own products, Pacs or projects is allowed in ordinary takes, **never in answers to questions or in question channels** (the post is refused and says why). A space's team can switch self-promotion off **for one channel or the whole space**; then those links can't be added there at all. The base rules say so.
- **Links (owner):** a link to **any AutoStacc page** (a take, a guide, a project, a creator, someone's profile) is welcome anywhere and shows as a warm **preview card**, not bare link text. **Product pages** are the exception: they count as promotion and follow the self-promotion rule. **Links to other sites** pass at once when the site is on **AutoStacc's safe list** (well-known music and creative sites) or the **space's own allowed list**; a site on the **site-wide ban list** (adult sites, sites that download automatically, malware and the like) is always refused, and no space can allow it; any other site **waits for the space's moderators**.
- **Automatic moderation (owner):** every space's team gets switchable, tunable rules: **word and phrase filters** (AutoStacc's own slur list is always on), **new-account rules** (first posts held, or no links, for accounts younger than a set age or new to the space), the **link rules** above, and **pattern rules** set up from a form (if a post matches, hold it, refuse it, tag it or reply with a message), like Reddit's AutoModerator.
- **Posting pace (owner):** each space's team sets its own **slow mode** (off, 30 s, 2 min, 10 min), counted per space; site-wide, about **3 new posts a minute** per person (like Reddit's rate limit), the same text twice in a row is refused, and daily caps are only a high safety net. Usernames (display names) can be changed in Settings, 5 times in 30 days (owner, 2026-10-27); posts can be edited for 24 hours with history; posts up to 10,000 characters.

## Built as independent features (owner, 2026-10-20)

"No system is reliant on another, so a major change needs no big rewrite" (owner). So:
- **A small core** (`server/community/core/`) owns spaces, channels, takes, posts, members, access checks, the formatter and the page frame, and knows nothing about any feature.
- **Each feature is a folder** (`server/community/features/<name>/`) with a manifest: its own switch (`community.<name>`), routes, tables (its own migration; it never alters core tables except through a column it owns, added in its own migration), browser script and styles (`js/community-<name>.js`, `css/community-<name>.css`) and tests (`test/api/community-<name>.suite.js`).
- **Features plug into the core only through named hooks**: events the core emits after it changes something (a take started, a reply posted, a post edited or removed, an answer picked), **slots** on the rendered pages (a post's footer, a take's tools, a space's header, a list row's badges, the front page's boxes), **formatter extensions** (embeds, store cards, code blocks, mentions add inline or block rules), and **checks** the core asks before a write (self-promotion, slow mode, blocks).
- **Features never import each other.** If two need the same thing, it moves into the core as a hook, or into a shared helper with no feature logic.
- **Switching a feature off** removes its routes (404), its slots render nothing, its formatter rules stop (links stay plain links), its checks stop, and its data stays untouched for when it's switched back on.
- **Loading (owner, 2026-10-21):** `js/community-kit.js` gives every feature `press(button, on)` (a request button's spinner, `GS.skeleton.busy`), `busy(form, on)` (every control off, spinner on the submit) and `deskWait()` (a Team desk placeholder that `deskPart()` replaces in place), [modules](modules.md#shared-helpers-every-feature-uses). Moderation's appeals, bans and team log and the report queue each hold a placeholder until their list arrives (removed if it fails). The moderated-notices box and a post's tools get none, since they are usually empty or small and a placeholder would shift the page. The community page Function (`server/community/core/page.js`) sends the `Speculation-Rules` header itself, since `_headers` doesn't reach Function responses.

## Spaces and channels

- **AutoStacc**: the platform's space. Channels at launch: *Announcements* (team only), *Help & questions*, *Feedback & ideas*, *Show your Stacc*, *Challenges*.
- **Project spaces**: the Official space of a project with the `"community"` feature (`data/projects.js` for first-party projects; a switch in the Studio for creators). Run by the creator team with their team roles ([builder](builder.md) teams).
- **Communities**: started by any verified account about a topic, or a creator (then Unofficial). Run by the founder and the moderators they appoint.

Owners create, rename, order and archive channels. Each channel sets who may start threads and which post types it allows.

**Addresses.** `/community` (spaces, Hot threads, filters), `/community/questions` (central Q&A: unanswered, answered, by tag), `/community/challenges` (open and past challenges everywhere), `/community/<space>`, `/community/<space>/<channel>`, `/community/t/<id>-<slug>`, `/community/rules`. A project space also appears at `/<creator>/<project>/community`, its canonical address. Space names are unique and permanent.

## Post as (owner, 2026-10-22, #205)

A space's team (AutoStacc's admins in its own space and in first-party project spaces; a creator's team in theirs) chooses per take and reply between their own community name and the space's name, with **Post as** on the forms. Team posts show the space's name with a Team badge, and the name shown is kept on the post (`as_team`, migration `0064_community_as_team.sql`); the server refuses it for anyone else (`team_only`). Brand names stay reserved as community names. The name step before a first post asks for a **username** (owner, 2026-10-27: "community name" confused people).

**Post as any name you speak for (owner, 2026-10-27):** besides the space's own name (`as: "space"`, its team only), anyone may post as any live creator or published project whose team they're on with the `community.update` capability (owner, admin, publisher), in any space, and AutoStacc's admins also as AutoStacc and the first-party creators and projects (so the AutoStacc creator's owner posts as AutoStacc even without admin) (`core/identities.js`; `GET /api/community/me` lists them as `identities`; forms send `as`, the older `asTeam: true` still means "space"). An unpublished project, a deleted or suspended creator, or a name not in your list is refused (`team_only`). The post keeps the name in `as_team` and the Team badge; the author's account stays recorded.

**Usernames (owner, 2026-10-27):** chosen when joining (`PUT /api/community/me`), optionally on Create account (`js/auth.js`: a username and the 16+ box, checked with `GET /api/community/names/check?name=` (60 per 10 minutes per client), kept in the browser by `GS.pendingUsername` until the account has a confirmed email, then joined on logging in, on the dashboard or in the community), and changed in Settings › Profile (`PATCH /api/community/me`, the same name rules, 5 per 30 days per account). Posts show the current name; an old name is free for others at once. Brand and creator names stay reserved: their teams use Post as. The new-take form offers only the channels and kinds the visitor may start (`GET /api/community/spaces/:id/mine` gives `team`, `mayUpdate` and the space's name).

## Managing a space (owner, 2026-10-27, #216; was the Team desk in the Studio, #205)

Each space is managed on its own page, **`/community/<space>/manage`** (a project space's at `/<creator>/<project>/community/manage`), never indexed, rendered the same for everyone (`manage` in `core/public.js`, `manageHook`); the browser fills it only for the space's team, and every action is checked on the server. The old `/studio?desk=<space id>` redirects there (`js/studio-desk.js`). Tabs from `js/community-desk.js`: **To do** (read-only notice, reports, posts waiting for approval, appeals; the tab counts what's waiting and says "All clear" otherwise), **Members** (the team, bans), **Space** (settings and channels, tags, look, welcome message, archive), **Rules** (slow mode, new members, filtered words, allowed sites, pattern rules, self-promotion, each with a one-line explanation) and **Log**. Moderators see only To do, Members and Log. Each feature's script hands its part over with a key that fixes its place, so nothing moves while parts load. On the space's own page the team sees only a slim "Manage" link with how many things wait in To do; the Studio's community switch links a project space's page. The top-bar bell counts community notices; desk items aren't in it yet.

**Who may do what** (`core/access.js`): a **moderator** (`isTeam`) moderates (To do, bans, the log); a **manager** (`isManager`: AutoStacc's admins for its own space and first-party project spaces, a creator's owner and admins for a project space, the founder for a community) also changes settings and rules (`teamSpace` with `write`, slow mode) and the team; anyone else gets 404, a moderator asking for a manager's action 403 `managers_only`. Being on a space's team grants nothing in the Studio or the Control Center.

**The team** (the **spaces** feature, `community.spaces`, `features/spaces/team.js`, `js/community-team.js`): `GET /api/community/spaces/:id/team`; managers appoint a moderator by username (`POST .../moderators`, up to 25, not someone banned there or suspended) and remove one (`DELETE .../moderators/:userId`), and a moderator may step down. Project spaces take these community-only moderators too, besides the creator's team. Only a community's founder hands it over, to one of its moderators (`POST .../hand-over`; the old founder stays a moderator), and archives or reopens it (`PUT .../archived`; archived stays readable, nothing new, and frees a place under the cap; reopening needs one). Every change is in the space's log.

## Starting communities

Built (owner, 2026-10-27, #215; the **spaces** feature, `features/spaces/start.js`, `js/community-start.js` on the front page):
- Any account with a confirmed email, a username and the 16+ confirmation, straight away, **no creator needed** (`POST /api/community/spaces`; `GET /api/community/start` says what's missing). The founder gets the `founder` role and `founder_user_id`; the space is kind `community`, Unofficial. Only the name is required: a description, rules and up to 8 channel names (default General, Help & questions, Off-topic) can be given or left for later. The founder lands on the space's management page. `GET /api/community/my-spaces` lists the spaces someone helps run.
- **A cap of 3 open communities per account** (archived ones don't count), set in the Control Center's Switches (`community_space_cap`, 0–99, audited), checked inside the write so a burst can't pass it. Creator and project spaces don't count toward it. Per-account exceptions aren't built.
- **Name checks**: the username rules (shape, reserved words such as AutoStacc, admin and official, creators' and projects' names unless they're yours; `spaceNameProblem` in `core/names.js`), unique among spaces; the address is made from the name and is permanent, and a few community words (`manage`, `t`, `rules`...) are refused. AutoStacc's slur list (#198) isn't in yet.
- **Inactive teams (automatic)**: when no team member has acted for **40 days**, or reports have waited unhandled for 40 days, the space turns read-only by itself and the founder is emailed. A team member reopens it by handling the queue. A long-read-only space can be taken over by a willing member (a request the system checks; no AutoStacc review).
- If a founder deletes their account, the longest-serving moderator becomes founder; with no moderators, it's archived (`founder_left`; built, `core/erase.js`). Handing an orphaned archived space to someone else isn't built yet (#139).
- Owner, for later: a system that makes starting a *project* "a little less willy-nilly" (not designed yet).

## Age, accounts and notifications

- **16+** to post, vote, start communities, add friends or enter challenges. Self-declared once (a confirmation in Settings and at first post). **No age-verification system** (owner). The terms say the person is responsible for what they declare. Reading stays open to all. An "underage account" report reason exists, because once AutoStacc knows someone is under 16 it must remove the account's community data; terms alone can't waive that. The terms wording needs the owner's (ideally a lawyer's) review.
- **Deleted accounts**: posts and answers stay, shown as "deleted user", so threads still make sense. Name, profile, points, friends and items are erased ([profiles](profiles.md)). The person can tick "delete my posts too".
- **Email notifications**, each switchable in Settings: answers to your questions, replies to you (grouped, at most every few hours), updates from projects you follow, friend and collab requests, challenge results. All of them configurable (owner).

## Design (architect review 2026-10-16)

**Project spaces and Updates (#138 part 1, built):** a project has a community when its `features` list holds `community`. A creator's owner or admin switches it on or off in the Studio (the **projectspaces** feature, `community.projectSpaces`: `PUT /api/community/projects/:id/space`, `js/studio-community.js`); a first-party project lists it in `data/projects.js` (Glitches & Stitches since 2026-10-22, owner; its nav links the space, the space's breadcrumbs lead back to the project, and AutoStacc's admins are its team, `core/access.js`). On, `core/project-space.js` makes the project's official space once (kind `project`, run by the creator's team through the project, never copied) with three channels (Announcements, team starts, Updates and discussions; General; Help & questions); off, it goes read-only (`state_reason` `community_off`) and nothing is deleted. The space's one address is `/<creator>/<project>/community` (and `/<channel>`), served by `core/project-routes.js` from the catch-all Function ahead of the creator pages; `/community/<space>` redirects there, and `spacePath` and the sitemap use the canonical address. **Updates** (`update` post type, in the core): official announcements, started only by people with the `community.update` capability (the platform's admins, a community's team, and a creator's owner, admins and publishers) in a channel that takes them. A project space shows to the public only while its project is live (published, not deleted, its creator active) and has the feature on (`SHOWN` in `core/spaces.js`, applied to the home page, lists, threads, sitemap, search, tags, similar questions, Saved and cards); a page already in the edge cache is served without database reads, the switch purges it, and looking at a page never reopens a closed space. Whether a creator at three copyright strikes keeps a public space is not decided. **Working proposals, not settled** (owner to confirm): the three default channels, and that "publishing capability" means the existing `community.update` role set. **Still to build for #138:** Updates on the project page and dashboards, polls from the Studio and communities, the central `/community/questions` page, and moving news (the news addresses still go to the project home, `project_news` rows are kept) once the owner says whether old news addresses render the Update or redirect to it. Tests: `test/api/community-projects.suite.js`.

**Names, team tools and the self-promotion rule (#161 part 1, built):** five independent features. **selfpromo** (`community.selfPromo`): a link to your own product, project or creator page (an AutoStacc address on a creator space you hold a role in) is refused in a question and in its answers, and in a whole space or channel once the team switches self-promotion off (`self_promo_answer`, `self_promo_off`); other people's links are untouched. **welcome** (`community.welcome`): the team's message (500 characters) shows once to each member on a first visit or first post. **look** (`community.look`): an accent colour and a banner from AutoStacc's presets only, applied in the browser. **teamtools** (`community.teamTools`): the team edits the space's description and rules and its channels (add, rename, who may start, kinds, archive; at least one stays), through `core/channels.js`. **profilecard** (`community.profileCard`): tapping a name shows name, month joined, best answers and the spaces they help run, with the blocks feature's Block button; reporting a person is done from one of their posts. Pinning and featuring a take is the moderation feature's pin. **Still to build for #161:** product takes, the buyer badge and release takes, which wait for project spaces (#138). Tests: `test/api/community-team.suite.js`.

**Finding (#160, built):** three independent features and the core's `terms.js` (words from what was typed: folded, common words dropped, at most six, LIKE wildcards escaped). **search** (`community.search`, `GET /api/community/search`): takes whose title or any showing reply has all the words, newest activity first, filtered by space, channel or kind; hidden or removed takes and removed replies find nothing. **tags** (`community.tags`): AutoStacc's starter list built from the store's own words (genre, style, DAW, plugin, contents, level; `features/tags/starter.js` from `server/discovery/vocabulary.js`) plus up to 20 each space's team adds; people pick, never type; up to 3 per take, set by the author for 24 hours or by the team; a tag lists its showing takes. **similar** (`community.similar`): while typing a new question's title, up to 5 visible questions that share at least half its words. Search, tags and similar read only the core's tables (plus tags' own); each works with the others off. Tests: `test/api/community-find.suite.js`.

**Keeping up (#159, built):** five independent features and one core piece. The core's `notify()` (`core/notify.js`, table `community_notifications`, migration 0055) is the one way a feature tells someone something: never yourself, never between people where either blocked the other, never a non-member, one notice per person and post (the more specific kind stays: a reply over "posted in a take you follow"). **follows** (`community.follows`): you follow your own takes and ones you reply to automatically, can follow or unfollow any take, and followers get a "followed" notice per reply. **inbox** (`community.inbox`): the bell (`js/community-inbox.js`) with replies to your takes and posts, mentions, best-answer picks and followed takes; only takes still showing; read notices go after 90 days and any after a year in the daily sweep. **mentions** (`community.mentions`): `@` and a community name (spaces allowed, the longest real name wins, up to 5 people a post) sends a notice; alerts can be switched off. **saved** (`community.saved`): a private Saved list. **joined** (`community.joined`): joined spaces' takes move to the top of the front page in the visitor's own browser (pages are cached and shared). The qa feature now emits `answerPicked` for the inbox. Each feature has its own switch, table(s), script and `erase`/`exportData`. Tests: `test/api/community-keepup.suite.js`.

**Held posts, link approval and automatic moderation (#158, built):** the core can hold a post. A feature's `post` check may answer `{ hold: { rule, reason } }` instead of refusing (`core/hooks.js`); the core then stores the take or reply hidden with a row in `community_holds` (migration 0050; `core/holds.js`, `core/holds-routes.js`), its writer sees it listed as waiting (`GET /api/community/holds/mine`, not as a takedown to appeal), and the space's team approves it (it appears, a held reply then counts toward the take's replies) or rejects it with a reason the author is told (removed under the space's rules). One decision wins (compare-and-set), at most 5 posts per person wait at once (checked inside the posting batch, so parallel requests can't exceed it), a team's other actions on a held post (hide, remove, restore, a legal removal) clear the hold, an approved post is announced like a new one (`threadStarted` or `replyPosted`, and its take moves up), a team can't allow an address covering many sites (`co.uk`, `github.io`), and an edit that would be held is refused (`needs_approval`) instead, since the old text is showing. Four independent rule features use it, each with its own switch, table, team-desk script and tests: **linkrules** (`community.linkRules`: AutoStacc's safe list and the space's allowed sites pass, the ban list is refused and no space can allow it, any other site holds the post; the lists are in `features/linkrules/lists.js`), **wordfilter** (`community.wordFilter`: AutoStacc's slur list, always on, refuses; the team's own words hold or refuse; whole words, accents and case folded), **newaccount** (`community.newAccount`: accounts younger than N days, or with fewer than N posts in the space, have their posts held or their links refused) and **patterns** (`community.patterns`: form-built "if a post matches, hold or refuse" rules from fixed tests, contains, starts with, has a link, mostly capitals, shorter than; plain comparisons only, never an expression). Team members' posts skip the holds, never the ban list or the slur list. **Not built yet:** the pattern actions "tag" (waits for tags, #160) and "reply with a message", and AutoStacc's slur list is empty until the owner supplies it. Tests: `test/unit/community-automod.test.js`, `test/api/community-automod.suite.js`.

**Moderation and reports (#137 part 1, built, switch off):** the core's `moderate.js` is the one place a take or post changes state for moderation (hidden, removed under a space's rules, visible again), compare-and-set in one guarded batch with its entry in `community_mod_log`; removals by the author or for a legal reason are never undone there. **Modules.** One area, `server/community/`, split by job: `spaces.js`, `access.js` (one check, scoped to a single space), `threads.js`, `posts.js`, `votes.js`, `format.js`, `render.js` and `public.js` (server-rendered pages), `sitemap.js`, `reports.js`, `moderation.js`, `erase.js` (account deletion and export), `routes.js`. It never imports `server/sites/` rendering, and orders, payments and entitlements never read community data. Wording: `server/library/` is the builder's "community library" of site pieces; the community is a different thing and keeps distinct names.

**Rendering.** Threads are user content, so they're rendered on the server by their own Pages Function for `/community` (planned, a thin entry calling the community area's public renderer), not the creators' catch-all, with the edge-cache pattern of `server/sites/public.js` (production, about a minute). The HTML is the same for everyone; votes and controls load from `/api/community/*`. Removing illegal content purges the cached page (one data centre per purge, so the cache stays short). Head: canonical, Open Graph, `DiscussionForumPosting` or `QAPage` JSON-LD. Thread URLs are listed in `/community/sitemap.xml` (a second `Sitemap:` line in `robots.txt`); hidden and removed threads are `noindex` and left out. User links carry `rel="ugc nofollow noopener"` and show their domain.

**Addresses.** A thread is `/community/t/<id>-<slug>`; the id is what counts, so an edited title redirects (301) to the new slug. Project spaces: in Studio projects, `community` can't be a page's address (refused since #135) and `server/sites/public.js` will hand that path to the community renderer. **Project spaces.** A project uses the community through its `"community"` feature; for first-party projects in `data/projects.js`, for creators a Studio switch that creates the space row. The team is the creator's team, read through the project, never copied.

**Formatting.** `server/community/core/format.js`: paragraphs, line breaks, **bold**, *italic*, `code`, lists, quotes, and http(s) links (bare or `[label](url)`), each link followed by its domain. Everything else is escaped; other link schemes, credentials in links and broken addresses stay plain text. Direction-control characters are dropped and labels sit in `<bdi>`, so a label can't reverse the shown domain (security review, 2026-10-16). For the read side: style `.link-host` so the domain is clearly separate and never cut off, and escape `plain()` wherever it goes into HTML (it returns raw text by design).

**Data (`0031_community.sql`).** `community_members` (display name, folded `name_key`, 16+ confirmation), `community_spaces` (kind platform / project / community, `project_id` for project spaces, state open / read_only / archived, last team activity), `community_space_roles` (founder, moderator), `community_channels` (allowed post types, who may start), `community_threads` and `community_posts` (state visible / hidden / removed and why, stored score and Helpful counters), `community_post_edits`, `community_votes`, `community_reports` (reason and route to the space or AutoStacc), `community_blocks`, `community_space_bans`, `community_suspensions`, and `community_mod_log` (append-only: what space teams did, so founders can see and undo it; AutoStacc's legal actions go to `admin_audit`). Authors become NULL ("deleted user") when an account is deleted, in code, since D1 doesn't enforce ON DELETE. Later migrations: tags, challenges, notification choices, strikes, profiles, points.

**Usernames** (display names) live in `community_members`, not the account name. The impersonation check will extend `server/creators/holds.js` to every creator, project, held name and reserved word, and fold look-alike characters first (today's `nameKey` drops non-Latin letters, so a Cyrillic "А" could slip past).

**Polls** from the community need a `source` column so the catalog sync (which owns `data/polls.js` polls) leaves them alone, and an owner that can be a space (`polls.project_id` is required today).

**Posting limits.** A confirmed email and the 16+ confirmation are required. New accounts get few threads, replies, votes and links per day (`server/lib/ratelimit.js`), and the limits loosen with age and a clean record. Authors can edit for a short window (with history) and delete their own posts.
