# Creators and the Studio

**A creator owns projects. A project has a page at `autostacc.com/<creator>/<project>`. Any account can become a creator for free and design its profile and its projects' sites in the Studio's visual builder, without code.** AutoStacc itself is the first creator (`autostacc`), and Glitches & Stitches is its project at `/autostacc/glitches-and-stitches`. Decisions: [decisions](decisions.md). Model: [architecture](architecture.md).

## Addresses

| Address | What |
| --- | --- |
| `/<creator>` | the creator's profile: the one they designed, else a list of their published projects |
| `/<creator>/<name>` | a project's home, else a page of the creator's profile (one address space per creator: a profile page can't take a project's name, nor a project a page's; `server/sites/addresses.js`) |
| `/<creator>/<project>` | the project's home page |
| `/<creator>/<project>/<page>` | another page of the project (`/events`, `/about`, ...); `/home` redirects to the project's home |

- **Names are unique.** A creator name is unique across AutoStacc and a project name is unique within its creator. Both are stored lowercase, so case never makes a second name; mixed-case addresses redirect to lowercase. A name is 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens.
- **Platform words are reserved** (`server/lib/slugs.js`: `shop`, `studio`, `admin`, `autostacc`, ...). A unit test fails if a top-level page, folder or Function of the site isn't reserved, so a new platform page can't be taken by a creator first. First-party creators in `data/creators.js` may use a reserved name (`firstParty: true`).
- **Nobody can pose as a first-party project.** A creator can't take the address or the display name of a first-party creator or project ("Glitches and Stitches", any case or punctuation).
- **Renames redirect.** Renaming a creator or project 301s the old address to the new one for 90 days. The old name stays held for that creator, so nobody else can grab it to impersonate them (`slug_holds`). Published pages are rendered again with the new address. At most 3 names are held per creator or project, so renames can't be used to squat names.
- **Adding a first-party creator:** add its id to `RESERVED` and deploy that first, then add it to `data/creators.js`. The build refuses a first-party creator whose id isn't reserved; otherwise a Studio account could take the name in between and the deploy's sync would stop on the clash.

## Two kinds of creators, one model

- **First-party** (`data/creators.js`, today only `autostacc`). Their projects' pages are hand-written in this repo (`<creator>/<project>.html`, `<creator>/<project>/`, a skin, sounds, scripts), the build generates the creator's profile (`<creator>.html`), and all of it is static. The deploy's catalog sync writes their rows (`source = 'catalog'`) and never touches anything else.
- **Studio creators** sign up in `/studio`. They exist only in the database (`source = 'studio'`), and their pages are rendered by the store (`server/sites/`) and served by the catch-all Function `functions/[[path]].js`.

Both live in D1 `creators`. Projects point at their creator (`projects.creator_id`), products at their project, and order lines at both. Creator-made products are `products.source = 'studio'` rows in the same tables (`created_by` records who made them); their store page is `/product?p=<id>` (`js/product-page.js`: who made it, tags, preview clip, Add to cart, Get it free, or STACC'D when already in Your Stacc, and for files published from the Pack Builder "What's inside" with the Pac's folders, read me and preview clips, `js/product-contents.js`), while first-party products keep `/shop/<id>`. They are listed in the store with AutoStacc's own: the store's search and filters (`/api/store/search`, [discovery](discovery.md)) read them from the database like any product, and the other pages' grids (`js/shop.js`) add the published Studio products from `/api/products`. The API marks each with `source`, `projectName`, `creator`, `projectPath` and `verified` (false until AutoStacc marks the creator **trusted** in the Control Center); cards and the product page then show an **Unverified creator** label, and the product page says AutoStacc hasn't verified the creator and checks files automatically to be what their names say, not for quality or rights (owner decision: all creators are listed, clearly marked; wording updated in Phase 9 now that packs are checked file by file). Creators can give a product **Store details** (genre, contents, formats, DAW, plugin, key, BPM, sample rate and bit depth, only the fields its kind has; `js/studio-details.js`), checked against the store vocabulary on the server and suggested from the product's own words but never applied for them, tag it and upload a public preview clip (`PUT`/`DELETE /api/studio/products/:id/preview`: MP3, M4A, OGG or WAV under 20 MB whose bytes match; stored as `previews/<product>-<random>.<ext>` so a replaced clip is never served from a cache, the old one deleted). Published Studio projects are listed on `/projects`, marked unverified the same way, and can be followed with Auto-Stacc (owner decision 2026-10-09, [browse](browse.md)); polls still need a feature only `data/projects.js` sets.

## The Studio (`/studio`)


Signed-in accounts with a confirmed email (the page is gated like `/dashboard`):

1. **Claim a creator name.** It's free with no plan or card, one creator per account for now.
2. **Create projects** (up to 10) and open one in the editor.
3. **Design the profile and each project's site in the visual builder** ([builder](builder.md)): start from a template or a blank page, then edit on the page itself (select, drag and resize on a 12-column grid, type into headings), with a side panel for each element's fields, style, motion and phone settings, sections with backgrounds, up to 12 pages with a menu, the look (style preset, accent, font or an uploaded WOFF2 font, logo), the creator's own CSS, undo and redo and a phone view. Elements: heading, text (paragraphs, **bold**, *italic*, links), button, image, gallery (with a lightbox), slideshow, music or video embed (YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music share links), an audio player for a product's preview clip, events, tabs, FAQ / accordion, countdown, social links, products, projects, a Follow button and a cart link (both links to AutoStacc's own pages), divider and box.
4. **Files:** upload PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP images and WOFF2 fonts. Delete one and its storage is freed at once, unless a published page or a kept version still uses it (then it stays until nothing does).
5. **Save the draft, preview it** (private, noindex) **and publish**, now or at a set time. The last 20 publishes are kept and can be restored. Unpublish takes the site offline (a profile falls back to the list of projects).
7. **Sell** (`server/studio/products.js`, `product-files.js`): products of its projects (up to 25 per creator; kinds, price range and payout hold in `SELLING`, `server/creators/policy.js`). A product goes on sale only with a deliverable file, of a type on the shared allow-list (`server/files/policy.js`, kept equal to the vocabulary's `FORMATS`). Paid files from a creator not yet trusted wait as `pending` until approved in the Control Center. A whole pack (a ZIP in folders) goes through the Pack Builder instead (**Upload a whole pack** on a product card, `js/studio-packs.js`, [packs](packs.md)): every file is checked, kinds, instruments, tempos and keys are suggested for review, and a clean version publishes at once while a flagged one, or every version of a creator the Control Center set to always hold, waits for AutoStacc's review (the automated review, `server/packs/review.js`). Uploads count against the creator's one storage cap (550 MB free, shared with their site files; one file in a Pac up to 4 GB, `LIMITS.pacFileMaxBytes`; files a revision replaced keep counting for the 30 days buyers can still download them, `server/creators/storage.js`), and stop for everyone while AutoStacc's own storage is at the cap the owner sets (`409 storage_full`, [packs](packs.md)); a version may also wait for next month's processing allowance. With the Pac editor on, one drop zone takes a single file, a folder or a whole ZIP, and it all opens in the visual builder (file cards, waveforms, drag and drop, folders, a read me, up to three preview clips) until the creator publishes; a whole Pac dropped onto one with files asks to replace it or add to it ([packs](packs.md#pac-editor-built-2026-10-15)). A new version of a pack is matched to the live one: unchanged and moved files keep their values, only changed files are uploaded, and buyers keep removed or replaced files for 30 days. Owners, admins and store managers manage products; only the owner sees earnings, connects a payout account and asks for payouts (`earnings.js`, `payout-account.js`; [payouts-setup](payouts-setup.md)).

Each product opens in its own workspace, the **Product Studio** (Overview, Pac Builder, Store Page, Pricing, Insights, Release; [studio](studio.md)); a project's Products page is a list of them with a short form for a new draft.

Code: `studio.html`, `js/studio.js` (overview), the visual builder (`js/builder*.js`, `js/site-*.js`, `css/builder*.css`; [builder](builder.md)), `js/studio-products.js` (a project's product list), `js/studio-workspace.js` and `js/studio-section-*.js` (the Product Studio), `js/studio-packs.js` (the Pack Builder) with the Pac editor's `js/pac-*.js`, `js/studio-details.js` (store details), `js/studio-earnings.js` (earnings and payouts), `css/studio.css`. API: `server/studio/` (`creators.js`, `sites.js`, `site-files.js`, `products.js`, `product-files.js`, `earnings.js`, `payout-account.js`, and for the Product Studio `workspace.js`, `pricing.js`, `insights.js`, `insights-data.js`); the Pack Builder's `/api/studio/packs/…` routes are `server/packs/` (`access.js`).

## Teams (built 2026-10-15)

A creator's owner (or an admin) invites people by email with a role (`server/studio/members.js`, `server/creators/team.js`, the Team panel `js/studio-team.js`): **Admin** (everything except money and deleting the creator), **Publisher** (edits and publishes the profile and sites), **Store manager** (products and their files, never money) and **Editor** (site drafts only). Routes ask for a capability, not a role (`server/creators/roles.js`: `site.edit`, `site.publish`, `products`, `projects`, `members`, `library.share`, `money`); a member without the capability gets the same 404 as a stranger. An invitation keeps only its token's hash, works once, for 7 days, and only for the account signed in with that confirmed address; an admin can't invite or change admins or touch the owner; anyone but the owner can leave. Invitations are limited per address, per account and per creator (each sends an email); the token travels in request bodies, never in addresses; an admin's pending invitations go when they stop being an admin or leave. Roles live in `creator_roles` beside `creator_members` (migration `0026_creator_team.sql`). Up to 10 people per team, invitations included (`LIMITS.membersPerCreator`). Deleting an account removes its memberships, roles and pending invitations to its address.

## How creator pages stay safe

- **No creator HTML or JavaScript; CSS only through a checker.** A site is a JSON document (`server/sites/document.js` accepts only its shape, drops unknown fields and refuses the rest with a 400 naming the place). Every tag is written by the shared renderer (`js/site-render.js`, `js/site-elements.js`), escaping all text first; inline styles come only from checked numbers and colours. A creator's CSS is parsed and rebuilt under the page area (`js/site-css.js`; [builder](builder.md#how-it-stays-safe)), and the page area is contained so nothing can be drawn over the AutoStacc strip. Links are only `https:`, `mailto:` or an AutoStacc page (never `/api/` or `/media/`). Off-site links get `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow ugc"`.
- **No logins, forms or payments on creator pages.** The CSP allows one script, AutoStacc's own runtime (`js/site-runtime.js`: motion, slideshows, tabs, lightbox, countdown, audio player; it reads checked data- attributes and makes no requests), and forbids forms, requests, frames other than the embed providers, and being framed: `default-src 'none'; script-src <site>/js/site-runtime.js; connect-src 'none'; form-action 'none'; frame-ancestors 'none'`. Every page carries a thin AutoStacc strip saying accounts, carts and payments only happen on AutoStacc itself. Product, cart and Follow elements link to AutoStacc's own pages, and buyers pay there.
- **Embeds are rebuilt, not pasted.** `embeds.js` parses a share link from a known provider and builds the player address from the parts it parsed. Anything else is refused.
- **Files are checked by their bytes.** The type comes from the file's first bytes, never from its name or the browser, so HTML or SVG can't pass as an image or font. Files are served from `/media/sites/<id>` with `nosniff`, a sandbox CSP and only while active, and never while the creator is suspended.
- **Ownership.** Only a creator's members see or change it. Anyone else gets a 404 (`requireCreator`, `requireProject`). A document may show only its creator's own images and products; anything else is left out at render time.
- **New creators aren't indexed.** Their pages are `noindex` until an admin marks the creator **trusted** in the Control Center (Creators tab), so spam can't ride on autostacc.com's reputation. Studio pages are not in `sitemap.xml` yet.
- **Suspension** (Control Center) takes a creator's pages and images offline within a minute (the edge and browser cache time), stops it from publishing and takes its products off sale (buyers keep what they own).
- **Limits hold under load.** The creator and project limits are checked inside the insert, so parallel requests can't exceed them.
- **The project directory, follows and Auto-Stacc** cover published Studio projects of active creators too (`server/projects/projects.js` `VISIBLE`); a suspended creator's projects drop out at once. Polls stay first-party.

Creator JavaScript is not allowed (owner, 2026-10-15): interactivity comes from AutoStacc's library instead. The earlier plan for sandboxed creator code on its own origin is kept in [creator pages](creator-pages.md) (not built).

## How they stay cheap

- A creator page is **not a deployment**. Publishing renders each page once into `site_pages`. A view is one indexed D1 read, cached at the edge for a minute in production (`server/sites/public.js`).
- Static pages never invoke the catch-all Function: `scripts/dist.js` writes `_routes.json` to exclude every static page and folder, first-party creators' folders and every `_redirects` source. Only unknown top-level addresses (Studio creators, typos) reach it.
- Free-plan limits live in one file (`server/creators/policy.js`): projects, pages, sections and elements per page, document and CSS size, image and font size and count, kept versions, and for selling products per creator and files per product. **One storage cap** covers everything a creator stores (550 MB, owner 2026-10-15: site images and fonts, paid files, Pac previews; `server/creators/storage.js`; extra storage will be paid by the creator, not built). The Control Center's storage quota (`storage_quota_bytes`) replaces it for one creator; an older paid-file quota set in the database (`files_quota_bytes`) does too, and the smaller of the two wins. The quota is checked before an upload is stored, and again atomically when it's recorded, so parallel uploads can't squeeze past it.

## Store foundations under creators

- **Seller and split per order line** (`server/revenue/split.js`). Each line records its project, creator, share of a promo code, net, platform fee and creator share, fixed when the order is written. The fee is the creator's own (admin-set, basis points) or the platform default, **20%** (`DEFAULT_PLATFORM_FEE_BPS` in `server/creators/policy.js`; owner, 2026-10-09). Changing it only affects later orders. Refunds are recorded on the order (`refunded_cents`) and, for full refunds, on each line. The Control Center's **Creators** statistics show fees and creators' shares. Nothing is paid out automatically: a creator's earnings and payout requests read these records (`server/revenue/earnings.js`, `payouts.js`), and the owner sends each payout from the Control Center ([payouts-setup](payouts-setup.md)).
- **Paid file lifecycle** (`server/files/`). Each paid file is `pending`, `published`, `rejected` or `deleted`, and only published, active files reach customers (downloads, Your Stacc, test-purchase checks). The admin can upload a file held back ("Hold back until I publish it") and publish it later. Uploads are limited by file type, size (95 MB) and count per product (`server/files/policy.js`). Creators' paid uploads use the same path (`server/studio/product-files.js`); packs uploaded through the Pack Builder pass the automated review instead (between checked and published, [packs](packs.md)).
- **Connected accounts** (`server/integrations/providers.js`, D1 `connected_accounts`). This is the extension point for Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and so on, with no provider switched on yet. The planned flow: OAuth with PKCE, the fewest read-only scopes, tokens encrypted before storage (or not kept at all), and the creator choosing what appears on their pages.

## Not built yet (on purpose)

- Per-project staff roles, paid extra storage, and per-creator tax forms. Selling and payouts are built: [payouts-setup](payouts-setup.md).
- Creator HTML and JavaScript (never, owner 2026-10-15), custom domains, more than one creator per account, reporting a page, Studio pages in the sitemap.
- Orphaned single-upload cleanup (Pack Builder uploads, versions and additions left unfinished are cleaned up by the daily sweep, [packs](packs.md)). Images are a single request (stored, then recorded; a failed record deletes the object). Removed paid files (deleted, rejected, or retired past their 30 days) have their bytes deleted a day later by the daily sweep (`server/files/retention.js`; the row stays, marked `purgedAt`), and count against the creator's quota until then, so deleting and uploading again can't fill AutoStacc's storage. A reconciliation job could still list the `products/<id>/` and `sites/<creator>/` prefixes against the database (not built).
