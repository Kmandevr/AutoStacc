# Creators and the Studio

**A creator owns projects. A project has a page at `autostacc.com/<creator>/<project>`. Any account can become a creator for free and design its profile and its projects' sites in the Studio's visual builder, without code.** AutoStacc itself is the first creator (`autostacc`), and Glitches & Stitches is its project at `/autostacc/glitches-and-stitches`. Decisions: [decisions](decisions.md). Model: [architecture](architecture.md).

## Addresses

| Address | What |
| --- | --- |
| `/<creator>` | the creator's profile: the one they designed, else a list of their published projects |
| `/creators` | the directory of creators (owner, 2026-10-21): every first-party creator and every active, published Studio creator with a published project, never a suspended or unpublished one, each with its projects and a link to its profile; it shows nothing the profile doesn't ([browse](browse.md#creators-creators)) |
| `/<creator>/<name>` | a project's home, else a page of the creator's profile (one address space per creator: a profile page can't take a project's name, nor a project a page's; `server/sites/addresses.js`) |
| `/<creator>/<project>` | the project's home page |
| `/<creator>/<project>/<page>` | another page of the project (`/events`, `/about`, ...); `/home` redirects to the project's home |

- **Names are unique.** A creator name is unique across AutoStacc and a project name is unique within its creator. Both are stored lowercase, so case never makes a second name; mixed-case addresses redirect to lowercase. A name is 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens.
- **Platform words are reserved** (`server/lib/slugs.js`: `shop`, `studio`, `admin`, `autostacc`, `guide`, `copyright`, `copyright-notice`, `community-rules`, and `community` / `forum` ahead of the planned [community](community.md), ...). Inside a project's site, `community` can't be a page's address either (`server/sites/document.js`). A unit test fails if a top-level page, folder or Function of the site isn't reserved, so a new platform page can't be taken by a creator first. First-party creators in `data/creators.js` may use a reserved name (`firstParty: true`).
- **Nobody can pose as a first-party project.** A creator can't take the address or the display name of a first-party creator or project ("Glitches and Stitches", any case or punctuation).
- **Renames redirect.** Renaming a creator or project 301s the old address to the new one for 90 days. The old name stays held for that creator, so nobody else can grab it to impersonate them (`slug_holds`). Published pages are rendered again with the new address. At most 3 names are held per creator or project, so renames can't be used to squat names.
- **Adding a first-party creator:** add its id to `RESERVED` and deploy that first, then add it to `data/creators.js`. The build refuses a first-party creator whose id isn't reserved; otherwise a Studio account could take the name in between and the deploy's sync would stop on the clash.

## Two kinds of creators, one model

- **First-party** (`data/creators.js`, today only `autostacc`). Their projects' pages are hand-written in this repo (`<creator>/<project>.html`, `<creator>/<project>/`, a skin, sounds, scripts), the build generates the creator's profile (`<creator>.html`), and all of it is static. The deploy's catalog sync writes their creator and project rows (`source = 'catalog'`, creators `trusted`, each project's look in `projects.look`) and never touches anything else. Since 2026-10-21 (owner, [decisions](decisions.md)) everything except that page code goes through the Studio like any creator's: their products and their projects' music and events are made there (below). Only the routes for those opt in to catalog creators (`requireCreator` / `requireProject` with `{ allowCatalog: true }`); the builder, profile site, renames, deletes, new projects, team and earnings stay closed to them. **Owner** (owner, 2026-10-21, #172): the verified account the deploy secret `FIRST_PARTY_OWNERS` names for that creator (`<creator>=<email>`, [store-setup](store-setup.md)) becomes its owner when it opens the Studio (`server/studio/creators.js`, audited; never while impersonated). No email or project is in the code. The earlier admin one-time claim is gone; whoever used it stays on the team as an admin (`0043_first_party_owners.sql`). A first-party creator doesn't count toward its owner's one-creator limit. **Money and limits** (owner, 2026-10-21, #171, #177): its sales are AutoStacc's own, 100% on the main Stripe account, with no fee, share or payout records and never held. It has no Studio limits and no paid storage (`isFirstParty`, `limitOf` in `server/creators/policy.js`, judged by the creator's source).
- **Studio creators** sign up in `/studio`. They exist only in the database (`source = 'studio'`), and their pages are rendered by the store (`server/sites/`) and served by the catch-all Function `functions/[[path]].js`.

Both live in D1 `creators`. Projects point at their creator (`projects.creator_id`), products at their project, and order lines at both. Creator-made products are `products.source = 'studio'` rows in the same tables (`created_by` records who made them); their store page is `/shop/<id>`, rendered on the server for every creator (`server/shop/`; owner, 2026-10-21, #175; the old `/product?p=<id>` answers a 301 there), with `js/product-page.js` for the interactive parts ( who made it, tags, preview clip, Add to cart, Get it free, or STACC'D when already in Your Stacc, and for files published from the Pack Builder "What's inside" with the Pac's folders, read me and preview clips, `js/product-contents.js`); there is no code-defined catalog any more (data/products.js was retired, #176), so first-party products are made in the Studio too. They are listed in the store with AutoStacc's own: the store's search and filters (`/api/store/search`, [discovery](discovery.md)) read them from the database like any product, and the other pages' grids (`js/shop.js`) add the published Studio products from `/api/products`. The API marks each with `source`, `projectName`, `creator`, `projectPath` and `verified` (false until AutoStacc marks the creator **trusted** in the Control Center); cards and the product page then show an **Unverified creator** label, and the product page says AutoStacc hasn't verified the creator and checks files automatically to be what their names say, not for quality or rights (owner decision: all creators are listed, clearly marked; wording updated in Phase 9 now that packs are checked file by file). Creators can give a product **Store details** (genre, contents, formats, DAW, plugin, key, BPM, sample rate and bit depth, only the fields its kind has; `js/studio-details.js`), checked against the store vocabulary on the server and suggested from the product's own words but never applied for them, tag it and upload a public preview clip (`PUT`/`DELETE /api/studio/products/:id/preview`: MP3, M4A, OGG or WAV under 20 MB whose bytes match; stored as `previews/<product>-<random>.<ext>` so a replaced clip is never served from a cache, the old one deleted). Published Studio projects are listed on `/projects`, marked unverified the same way, and can be followed with Auto-Stacc (owner decision 2026-10-09, [browse](browse.md)); polls still need a feature only `data/projects.js` sets.

## The Studio (`/studio`)


Signed-in accounts with a confirmed email (the page is gated like `/dashboard`):

1. **Claim a creator name.** It's free with no plan or card, one creator per account for now (unless the owner has closed new creators in Control Center › Settings › Switches).
2. **Create projects** (up to 10) and open one in the editor.
3. **Design the profile and each project's site in the visual builder** ([builder](builder.md)): start from a template or a blank page, then edit on the page itself (select, drag and resize on a 12-column grid, type into headings), with a side panel for each element's fields, style, motion and phone settings, sections with backgrounds, up to 12 pages with a menu, the look (style preset, accent, font or an uploaded WOFF2 font, logo), the creator's own CSS, undo and redo and a phone view. Elements: heading, text (paragraphs, **bold**, *italic*, links), button, image, gallery (with a lightbox), slideshow, music or video embed (YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music share links), an audio player for a product's preview clip, releases, events (typed in or a project's own), tabs, FAQ / accordion, countdown, social links, products, projects, a Follow button and a cart link (both links to AutoStacc's own pages), divider and box.
4. **Files:** upload PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP images and WOFF2 fonts. Delete one and its storage is freed at once, unless a published page or a kept version still uses it (then it stays until nothing does).
5. **Save the draft, preview it** (private, noindex) **and publish**, now or at a set time. The last 20 publishes are kept and can be restored. Unpublish takes the site offline (a profile falls back to the list of projects).
7. **Sell** (`server/studio/products.js`, `product-files.js`): products of its projects (up to 25 per creator; kinds, price range and payout hold in `SELLING`, `server/creators/policy.js`). A product goes on sale only with a deliverable file, of a type on the shared allow-list (`server/files/policy.js`, kept equal to the vocabulary's `FORMATS`). Paid files from a creator not yet trusted wait as `pending` until approved in the Control Center. A whole pack (a ZIP in folders) goes through the Pack Builder instead (**Upload a whole pack** on a product card, `js/studio-packs.js`, [packs](packs.md)): every file is checked, kinds, instruments, tempos and keys are suggested for review, and a clean version publishes at once while a flagged one, or every version of a creator the Control Center set to always hold, waits for AutoStacc's review (the automated review, `server/packs/review.js`). Uploads count against the creator's one storage cap (550 MB free, shared with their site files; one file in a Pac up to 4 GB, `LIMITS.pacFileMaxBytes`; files a revision replaced keep counting for the 30 days buyers can still download them, `server/creators/storage.js`), and stop for everyone while AutoStacc's own storage is at the cap the owner sets (`409 storage_full`, [packs](packs.md)); a version may also wait for next month's processing allowance. With the Pac editor on, one drop zone takes a single file, a folder or a whole ZIP, and it all opens in the visual builder (file cards, waveforms, drag and drop, folders, a read me, up to three preview clips) until the creator publishes; a whole Pac dropped onto one with files asks to replace it or add to it ([packs](packs.md#pac-editor-built-2026-10-15)). A new version of a pack is matched to the live one: unchanged and moved files keep their values, only changed files are uploaded, and buyers keep removed or replaced files for 30 days. Owners, admins and store managers manage products; only the owner sees earnings, connects a payout account and asks for payouts (`earnings.js`, `payout-account.js`; [payouts-setup](payouts-setup.md)).

8. **Music and events** (below): each project's releases and events.

Each product opens in its own workspace, the **Product Studio** (Overview, Pac Builder, Store Page, Pricing, Insights, Release; [studio](studio.md)); a project's Products page is a list of them with a short form for a new draft.

Code: `studio.html`, `js/studio.js` (overview), the visual builder (`js/builder*.js`, `js/site-*.js`, `css/builder*.css`; [builder](builder.md)), `js/studio-products.js` (a project's product list), `js/studio-workspace.js` and `js/studio-section-*.js` (the Product Studio), `js/studio-packs.js` (the Pac Builder: draft, ready-to-publish, published, failed or not-accepted versions, each with what to do next) with the Pac editor's `js/pac-*.js`, `js/studio-details.js` (store details), `js/studio-earnings.js` (earnings and payouts), `css/studio.css`. API: `server/studio/` (`creators.js`, `sites.js`, `site-files.js`, `products.js`, `product-files.js`, `earnings.js`, `payout-account.js`, and for the Product Studio `workspace.js`, `pricing.js`, `insights.js`, `insights-data.js`); the Pack Builder's `/api/studio/packs/…` routes are `server/packs/` (`access.js`).

## Music and events

Built 2026-10-21; shows became events and news was hidden the same day ([decisions](decisions.md), #174, #178).

Every creator, first-party included, keeps each project's releases and events in the Studio (owner, 2026-10-21). A project's card has **Music** and **Events** buttons (`js/studio-content.js` with `js/studio-music.js` and `js/studio-events.js`, `#content=<projectId>&kind=music|events`). "Music" because the Product Studio already has a **Release** section, which publishes a product. **News is hidden** until each creator's community opens. Its posts belong on the creator's Community page, so there is no News editor, and its rows wait in D1 (`project_news`) ([community](community.md)).

- **Stored** in D1 `project_releases` and `project_events` (`0040_project_content.sql`; `0041_events.sql` renamed `project_shows` and added an event's slug, title, start time, last day, time zone, country, tags and description, and tags on releases). A project can have up to 200 releases and 200 events. Each item is a draft until published, and deleting one is a soft delete. A slug is unique within its project and never changes once published, because it is the address.
- **Checked on the server** (`server/content/clean.js`):
  - Text is plain; a description is the safe markup of `js/site-text.js` (never HTML).
  - Links are https only.
  - The player is a link `js/site-embeds.js` knows.
  - Artwork is one of the creator's own uploaded site images, with alt text.
  - Tags follow the store's one rule (`server/lib/tags.js`: up to 8, lowercase words joined by hyphens, 24 characters each).
  - An event's time zone is an IANA name, and its country is a two-letter code.
  - Values migrated from `data/` that these rules would refuse (HAIL's `/img/releases/` artwork until it is moved, [admin](admin.md); a Deezer widget) are kept only while left unchanged.
- **Roles:** writing a draft needs `site.edit`; publishing, unpublishing, changing a live item and deleting need `site.publish` (`server/content/routes.js`; existing capabilities, no new roles).
- **API:**
  - Studio: `GET`/`POST /api/studio/projects/:projectId/{releases|events}` and `PATCH`/`DELETE /api/studio/{releases|events}/:id`, rate-limited.
  - Public: `GET /api/projects/:projectId/content` (one project's published releases and upcoming events, `{ releases, events }`) and `GET /api/content/latest`, read in the browser by `js/project-content.js` (`GS.content`). `GET /api/events` returns the calendar's events with the same filters as `/events`. With `following=1` it returns only the projects the signed-in account follows; that answer is private and never cached. Only published content of visible projects is shown.
- **Pages** (`server/content/`, via `functions/[[path]].js` before the product and Studio site pages), the same for every creator:
  - A release at `/<creator>/<project>/music/<slug>`.
  - A project's events list at `/<creator>/<project>/events`. A first-party project has it when it opts into the `events` feature; for a Studio project, its own published site page `events` wins.
  - Each event at `/<creator>/<project>/events/<slug>`, with Event JSON-LD and **Add to calendar**: an `.ics` download at `<slug>.ics` and a Google Calendar link. Nothing is collected.
  - The public events calendar `/events` (`server/content/calendar.js`, linked from Explore). It shows a month or a list of event cards, filtered by dates, city, country and host. Signed in, a Following filter appears (`js/events-calendar.js`). Phones get the list, and the month table works by keyboard.
  - The old `/<creator>/<project>/news` and `/news/<slug>` answer a 301 to the project's home.
  - Each page gets a title, canonical, Open Graph and JSON-LD. It is `noindex` unless the creator is trusted (`/events` is always indexable). A project's look comes from `projects.look` (never creator code). Pages are cached at the edge for a minute. Published pages of trusted creators and `/events` are listed in `/sitemap-content.xml` (`server/content/sitemap.js`; [seo](seo.md)).
- **Directory activity** (`/projects`) reads the dates of published releases and the next event from here, for every project.
- **On creator pages:** the visual builder's **Releases** and **Events** elements show a creator's own project's releases or upcoming events ([builder](builder.md)).

## Teams (built 2026-10-15)

A creator's owner (or an admin) invites people by email with a role (`server/studio/members.js`, `server/creators/team.js`, the Team panel `js/studio-team.js`): **Admin** (everything except money and deleting the creator), **Publisher** (edits and publishes the profile and sites), **Store manager** (products and their files, never money) and **Editor** (site drafts only). Routes ask for a capability, not a role (`server/creators/roles.js`: `site.edit`, `site.publish`, `products`, `projects`, `members`, `library.share`, `community.moderate`, `community.update`, `money`); a member without the capability gets the same 404 as a stranger. An invitation keeps only its token's hash, works once, for 7 days, and only for the account signed in with that confirmed address; an admin can't invite or change admins or touch the owner; anyone but the owner can leave. Invitations are limited per address, per account and per creator (each sends an email); the token travels in request bodies, never in addresses; an admin's pending invitations go when they stop being an admin or leave. Roles live in `creator_roles` beside `creator_members` (migration `0026_creator_team.sql`). Up to 10 people per team, invitations included (`LIMITS.membersPerCreator`). Deleting an account removes its memberships, roles and pending invitations to its address.

Trusted creators share elements, sections and whole sites with every creator, free or for sale as `template` products; details, storage and takedowns in [builder](builder.md#community-library-built).

## How creator pages stay safe

- **No creator HTML or JavaScript; CSS only through a checker.** A site is a JSON document (`server/sites/document.js` accepts only its shape, drops unknown fields and refuses the rest with a 400 naming the place). Every tag is written by the shared renderer (`js/site-render.js`, `js/site-elements.js`), escaping all text first; inline styles come only from checked numbers and colours. A creator's CSS is parsed and rebuilt under the page area (`js/site-css.js`; [builder](builder.md#how-it-stays-safe)), and the page area is contained so nothing can be drawn over the AutoStacc strip. Links are only `https:`, `mailto:` or an AutoStacc page (never `/api/` or `/media/`). Off-site links get `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow ugc"`.
- **No logins, forms or payments on creator pages.** The CSP allows one script, AutoStacc's own runtime (`js/site-runtime.js`: motion, slideshows, tabs, lightbox, countdown, audio player; it reads checked data- attributes and makes no requests), and forbids forms, requests, frames other than the embed providers, and being framed: `default-src 'none'; script-src <site>/js/site-runtime.js; connect-src 'none'; form-action 'none'; frame-ancestors 'none'`. Every page carries a thin AutoStacc strip saying accounts, carts and payments only happen on AutoStacc itself. Product, cart and Follow elements link to AutoStacc's own pages, and buyers pay there.
- **Embeds are rebuilt, not pasted.** `embeds.js` parses a share link from a known provider and builds the player address from the parts it parsed. Anything else is refused.
- **Files are checked by their bytes.** The type comes from the file's first bytes, never from its name or the browser, so HTML or SVG can't pass as an image or font. Files are served from `/media/sites/<id>` with `nosniff`, a sandbox CSP and only while active, and never while the creator is suspended.
- **Ownership.** Only a creator's members see or change it. Anyone else gets a 404 (`requireCreator`, `requireProject`). A document may show only its creator's own images and products; anything else is left out at render time.
- **New creators aren't indexed.** Their pages are `noindex` until an admin marks the creator **trusted** in the Control Center (Creators tab), so spam can't ride on autostacc.com's reputation. Studio site pages are not in a sitemap yet. Release and event pages of trusted creators are, in `/sitemap-content.xml`, and their product pages in `/sitemap-shop.xml`.
- **Suspension** (Control Center) takes a creator's pages and images offline within a minute (the edge and browser cache time), stops it from publishing and takes its products off sale (buyers keep what they own).
- **Limits hold under load.** The creator and project limits are checked inside the insert, so parallel requests can't exceed them.
- **The project directory, follows and Auto-Stacc** cover published Studio projects of active creators too (`server/projects/projects.js` `VISIBLE`); a suspended creator's projects drop out at once. Polls stay first-party.

Creator JavaScript is not allowed (owner, 2026-10-15): interactivity comes from AutoStacc's library instead. The earlier plan for sandboxed creator code on its own origin is kept in [creator pages](creator-pages.md) (not built).

## How they stay cheap

- A creator page is **not a deployment**. Publishing renders each page once into `site_pages`. A view is one indexed D1 read, cached at the edge for a minute in production (`server/sites/public.js`).
- Static pages never invoke the catch-all Function: `scripts/dist.js` writes `_routes.json` to exclude every static page and folder, each first-party creator's hand-written pages and files by their exact address, and every `_redirects` source. Only Studio creators' addresses, every project's `/music/<slug>`, `/events[/<slug>]` and old `/news` addresses, the `/events` calendar, `/shop/<id>`, `/product`, and typos reach it.
- Free-plan limits live in one file (`server/creators/policy.js`; first-party creators have none, #177): projects, pages, sections and elements per page, document and CSS size, image and font size and count, kept versions, and for selling products per creator and files per product. **One storage cap** covers everything a creator stores (550 MB, owner 2026-10-15: site images and fonts, paid files, Pac previews; `server/creators/storage.js`, which counts each stored original once). More is [paid storage](#paid-storage-built-2026-10-16-off), off until the owner switches it on. The Control Center's storage quota (`storage_quota_bytes`) replaces it for one creator; an older paid-file quota set in the database (`files_quota_bytes`) does too, and the smaller of the two wins. The quota is checked before an upload is stored, and again atomically when it's recorded, so parallel uploads can't squeeze past it.

## Paid storage (built 2026-10-16, off)

Storage above the free allowance, paid by the creator. **Switched on by the owner on 2026-10-21** in the Control Center (**Paid storage**), after allowing saved cards and adding the two webhook events in Stripe and a test-mode payment that worked (the heading's "off" is kept so links to it hold); the rate is the owner's, **$0.05 per GB a month** (owner, 2026-10-16; store setting `storage_rate`, default `500` hundredths of a cent). Kept apart from the budget guard (`server/usage/`), which protects AutoStacc's own Cloudflare bill, with one link (owner, 2026-10-21, #142): **the platform cap grows by the paid limits creators hold**. The effective cap is the owner's base (`storage_cap_gb`, default 9 GB) plus, for every active plan of a non-first-party creator, its limit above that creator's free storage (`paidCapBytes` in `server/usage/storage.js`, `capParts` in `server/usage/usage.js`). Only the part above free storage is added, because free storage is already expected inside the base; counting whole limits would count it twice. Every upload guard uses the effective cap, so paying creators never meet the site-wide wall; a past-due or cancelled plan stops adding room. The budget guard is otherwise unchanged.

- **What counts** (`server/creators/storage.js`, `PARTS`): single paid files, stored originals (each once, however many products, versions, folders or library views use it; files a revision replaced, for their 30 days), Pac uploads in progress, preview clips, site images and fonts. Never AutoStacc's own copies (deflated ZIP variants, waveforms, cached bundles). The same sum checks uploads, is shown in the Studio (split by part) and is billed. Before 2026-10-16 a file two products shared, or one re-added with the same bytes, counted twice; rejected Pac files' originals didn't count at all.
- **A monthly cap in dollars** (owner, 2026-10-16; `server/billing/plans.js`, `storage_plans`): the owner of a creator space says the most they'll spend a month ($1 to $100). At the rate that buys an upload limit (`capBytesFor`: free storage plus what the cap pays for a whole month; $1 = 20 GB), so uploads stop before a month can cost more than the cap. `quotaOf` is the larger of the free storage and `creators.storage_paid_bytes`, written only while the plan is active. A new rate works every plan's limit out again (logged per creator). What's charged is what's stored, never unused room.
- **Over the limit** (a lowered cap, or cancelled): uploads stop and storage above the free allowance is **still charged until it's back under** (owner, 2026-10-16), with a notice in the Studio linking to support (`/contact`) to fix it or remove files. Cancelling removes the limit at once; once a day's measurement finds the creator within the free storage the plan `ended`. So nobody can fill storage under a cap and cancel to keep it free.
- **Measured once a day** (`server/billing/statements.js`, the daily sweep, after every cleanup): each creator with a plan that hasn't ended gets one row in `storage_usage_days`: what's stored, split by part, the free storage, what's above it, and the rate that day (none while billing is off). A day without a row is never charged.
- **Monthly statements** (`storage_statements`, one per creator and month, issued once after the month ends): each day is 1/(days in the month) of a GB-month at its rate, summed exactly (`server/billing/policy.js`, integers only) and rounded once. **Earnings first** (owner, 2026-10-16): the statement is settled from the creator's earnings that have cleared the 14-day hold (never money on hold), recorded once in `earnings_deductions` (`server/revenue/deductions.js`, counted like money paid out but shown separately); the **saved card is charged the rest** automatically (below). A remainder under $0.50 (the card minimum) is carried into the next month's statement. Due 14 days after it's issued; the owner is emailed (`storage-billing`). Earnings and a payout request asked for at the same moment can both take the same money; the payout's balance then goes negative and comes out of later sales, as with refunds.
- **Saved card** (owner, 2026-10-21, #142; `server/billing/cards.js`, `storage_cards`, migration `0044_storage_cards.sql`): the owner of a creator space saves a card through a Stripe Checkout page in setup mode on the **main** account, with one Stripe Customer per creator space (AutoStacc gives Stripe the owner's email and the space's name). Card details never reach AutoStacc: it keeps the Stripe customer id, the payment method id, and the brand and last 4 shown in the Studio. A card is recorded only from a verified `checkout.session.completed` in setup mode (or the lookup made when the owner comes back from the page), matching the account, live/test mode, creator, customer and the latest setup session, compare-and-set; replacing a card detaches the old one at Stripe. Removing it detaches it at Stripe first (a refusal changes nothing and says so); with a plan still active, later statements fall back to the pay page. Owner only, refused for first-party creators, while billing is off and while impersonating; each step is in the billing log (`card.setup_started`, `.saved`, `.removed`), with brand and last 4 only. Saving a card again re-schedules statements that were waiting on one.
- **Automatic charges** (owner, 2026-10-21, #142; `server/billing/charges.js`, Stripe calls in `server/payments/stripe-cards.js`): after earnings, the daily sweep charges what's left of an open statement (at least $0.50) off-session to the saved card, on the day it's issued and, after a decline, again on days 3, 7 and 12 (`BILLING.chargeDays` and `nextChargeAt` in `server/billing/policy.js`, all inside the 14-day grace). Each try is claimed compare-and-set on the attempt count, so two sweeps at once charge once, and Stripe's idempotency key is per statement and attempt. A charge Stripe answers as succeeded is recorded at once as a verified payment; its `payment_intent.succeeded` webhook then finds it already paid. A `payment_intent.payment_failed` (only for charges carrying the storage charge marker, so a pay-page payment never routes twice) is handled the same way as a decline answered at once, once per attempt. Each decline emails the owner (`storage-billing`, stage `charge_failed`) with the next try's date and a link to update the card or pay. A bank asking the owner to confirm (SCA) stops the off-session tries and emails a pay link (`action_required`); no card emails the statement with the pay link (`issued`). After 14 days unpaid the plan goes `past_due` as before. The decline code is kept, never card data. While a charge is in progress the pay page refuses for an hour (`409 charge_in_progress`), so the two rarely both pay; if they do, the second is refused for the admins to refund, as before. Known limit: the refusal covers only a charge in progress. A pay page opened while a statement is `declined` or `no_card` stays payable after the sweep puts the statement back in line (for example once a card is saved) and charges the card; the charge does not check for an open pay page first, so both can succeed, and the second is refused and the admins are alerted to refund it.
- **Pay page, the fallback** (`server/billing/payments.js`): for no card, a declined card, or a bank asking for confirmation, a Checkout page on AutoStacc's **main** Stripe account (storage is AutoStacc's own service; the owner left the account to Claude), one line at what's left with `STORAGE_TAX_CODE` (`server/payments/tax-codes.js`, general for now). Paid only by a verified event (`server/payments/events.js`, once per event id) matching the statement (its session, or the statement id an earlier page carries), account, live/test mode, amount and currency; compare-and-set, so duplicates and late events do nothing, and a second payment of the same statement is refused for the admins to refund. Before opening a new page the last one is looked up: if it was just paid, that's recorded instead. Each request has its own key, so two clicks at once can't close each other's page.
- **Overdue** (after the 14 days) or **disputed** (a chargeback): the plan is `past_due`, the paid limit is withdrawn, so new uploads stop above the free storage. Nothing stored is deleted, hidden or unpublished for billing, ever (owner, 2026-10-16: only uploads pause); buyers keep their downloads. Paying, a waiver or a won dispute restores the limit; a bigger cap waits until it's settled. A lost dispute leaves the statement `disputed` (unpaid).
- **Credits and refunds** (Control Center): waive what's left of an open, carried or disputed statement, or refund a paid one in full (the card part through Stripe, the earnings part back to earnings; a refused refund changes nothing and says so). Refund events are scaled back to before tax like orders' (`server/payments/refund-amount.js`). A note is required; both are audited and land in the creator's own log.
- **Seeing it** (`GET /api/studio/creators/:id/storage`, `js/studio-storage.js`): every member sees the storage by part against the limit; only the owner sees the plan, the saved card (`js/studio-storage-card.js`), this month so far, statements (with the part paid from earnings), every day behind a statement and the billing log (`storage_billing_log`). The Control Center shows plans, statements (overdue and disputed marked), the log, one creator's days for a dispute, and the platform cap as base + paid = effective.
- **Evasion**: one creator space per account (`LIMITS.creatorsPerAccount`); files count once per creator, never zero (shared pieces count against the seller, released files until deleted); revisions count for their 30 days; cancelling keeps charging above the allowance. Several accounts can't be stopped outright.
- **Tax** (owner, 2026-10-21, #142): the general digital code stays (`STORAGE_TAX_CODE`, `server/payments/tax-codes.js`), **to confirm with a tax adviser later**. An off-session charge is a PaymentIntent with no line items, so the tax code applies only on the pay page; whether Stripe's Managed Payments allows and taxes off-session charges on the main account is checked in the owner's test-mode run ([store-setup](store-setup.md#paid-storage-saved-cards)). If it doesn't, the alternative is Stripe Invoices with tax codes.
- **Still off.** Built, not switched on: the owner saves cards and runs one real test-mode payment end to end, then switches it on in the Control Center ([store-setup](store-setup.md#paid-storage-saved-cards)). Still open: anything beyond pausing uploads for statements unpaid for months.
- Tests: `test/unit/storage-billing.test.js` (arithmetic, earnings first, caps), `test/api/storage-billing.suite.js` (payments group: counting once, caps, rate changes, measuring, statements, carrying, earnings first, paying in mock and Stripe mode, earlier pages, duplicates, mismatches, overdue, disputes, cancelling, waivers, refunds, other creators and members), plus saved cards and automatic charges in the same group (`test/api/storage-cards.suite.js`; fake Stripe in `test/support/fake-stripe.js`: saving, off-session success, declines, a bank asking for confirmation, retries, duplicate events, two sweeps at once).

## Store foundations under creators

- **Seller and split per order line** (`server/revenue/split.js`). Each line records its project, creator, share of a promo code, net, platform fee and creator share, fixed when the order is written. A first-party creator's line has no fee and no share: it is AutoStacc's own revenue and never reaches earnings or payouts (owner, 2026-10-21, #171). For other creators the fee is the creator's own (admin-set, basis points) or the platform default, **20%** (`DEFAULT_PLATFORM_FEE_BPS` in `server/creators/policy.js`; owner, 2026-10-09). Changing it only affects later orders. Refunds are recorded on the order (`refunded_cents`) and, for full refunds, on each line. The Control Center's **Creators** statistics show fees and creators' shares. Nothing is paid out automatically: a creator's earnings and payout requests read these records (`server/revenue/earnings.js`, `payouts.js`), and the owner sends each payout from the Control Center ([payouts-setup](payouts-setup.md)).
- **Three copyright strikes:** the Studio refuses every change from that account, except deleting, payouts, the payout account and paying storage statements (routes marked `{ copyright: "allow" }`); reading stays open ([copyright](copyright.md)).
- **Copyright strikes** ([copyright](copyright.md)): an account at two strikes can't create, price or publish products or start a sale in any creator space (`copyright_paused`); a creator space at two has its products held in carts, refused at checkout and for free claims, and skipped by Auto-Stacc.
- **Held files:** a file a copyright notice took down (`held_at`) reaches nobody, past buyers included, until a counter-notice brings it back; a product left without files goes back to draft ([copyright](copyright.md)).
- **Paid file lifecycle** (`server/files/`). Each paid file is `pending`, `published`, `rejected` or `deleted`, and only published, active files reach customers (downloads, Your Stacc, test-purchase checks). The admin can upload a file held back ("Hold back until I publish it") and publish it later. Uploads are limited by file type, size (95 MB) and count per product (`server/files/policy.js`). Creators' paid uploads use the same path (`server/studio/product-files.js`); packs uploaded through the Pack Builder pass the automated review instead (between checked and published, [packs](packs.md)).
- **Connected accounts** (`server/integrations/providers.js`, D1 `connected_accounts`). This is the extension point for Spotify, SoundCloud, YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and so on, with no provider switched on yet. The planned flow: OAuth with PKCE, the fewest read-only scopes, tokens encrypted before storage (or not kept at all), and the creator choosing what appears on their pages.
