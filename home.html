About

Glitches & Stitches is an electronic music project built on one idea: an unfinished song isn't a failure, it's raw material. A great drop, a stray melody, a weird texture, a happy accident. Nothing gets thrown away.

The glitches are the mistakes, artifacts, chops and corrupted sounds. The stitches are what hold the fragments together. Every track becomes a part for the next one, so the catalog keeps feeding itself: remix, then remix².

Experimental doesn't mean random. However chaotic it sounds, it's built on purpose.

The loop

Every finished track is a parts bin for the next one.

  1. Create Start a song or an idea.
  2. Finish Get it far enough to join the catalog.
  3. Extract Pull out the best pieces: a drop, a melody, a texture.
  4. Stitch Sew those pieces into another track.
  5. Glitch Chop, corrupt and reinterpret them.
  6. Stitch it all Combine whole previous tracks. Remix².
  7. Repeat Feed it back into the library and go again.
