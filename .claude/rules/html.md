# Pages

- The frame and footer are empty `<header id="site-header">` / `<footer id="site-footer">` elements that a script fills: `js/layout.js` on platform pages (sidebar, top bar), `js/project-frame.js` on a project's pages (AutoStacc strip, the project's own header and footer). Never hand-write them. `<body data-page="...">` marks the current platform page; a project's pages also carry `data-project="<id>" data-project-page="<page>"`.
- `js/theme.js` loads in `<head>` on every page (no flash of the wrong theme). Load only what the page uses, in this order: `data/site.js`, `data/projects.js`, other `data/*.js`, `js/util.js`, `js/layout.js` (or `js/project-frame.js` on a project's pages), `js/account-nav.js`, then feature scripts. Stylesheets: `core`, `platform`, `layout` (plus `project-frame` on a project's pages), then feature sheets, then the project's skin. UI sounds and easter eggs (`js/sound.js`, `eggs.js`, `terminal.js`, `css/eggs.css`) only on pages of a project with `sounds: true`. Add a feature's script, data file and stylesheet to the pages that use it, and no others.
- Paths are root-relative and links use clean addresses as served in production: `/css/core.css`, `/shop`, `/autostacc/glitches-and-stitches/music/hail`, `/` for the home page. Never `shop.html` (that's a redirect hop). Preview locally with `node scripts/serve.js`.
- The home page is AutoStacc's public cover, `index.html` (served at `/`); its title and description come from `data/site.js`. The signed-in pages (dashboard, settings, Your Stacc, studio) are separate pages; Settings is one page served at each `/settings/<section>`. A project's pages live in `<creator>/<id>.html` (its home, title and description from `data/projects.js`) and `<creator>/<id>/*.html`, with `<main class="skin-<id>">` when it has a skin; platform pages use `<main class="platform">`.
- A new platform page is a copy of `about.html` with its own `<title>` (ending `· AutoStacc`); a new project page is a copy of one of that project's pages (title `<page> · <project> · AutoStacc`). The hub's title starts with "AutoStacc" instead; the separator after it ("·" or "—") is grammar, not a rule (owner, 2026-10-09; the home page's title comes from `data/site.js`; `seo-test.js` accepts any title that starts with "AutoStacc"). Platform pages wear the AutoStacc look (light and dark, no sounds); a project's pages wear its skin and sounds. Each has meta description, `data-page`, the two empty seo markers and one `<h1 class="glitch">`. Then run `node scripts/build.js`: it fills canonical, Open Graph, Twitter and JSON-LD and adds the page to `sitemap.xml`. Link it from the nav (`js/layout.js`, or the project's `pages` in `data/projects.js`) or from related pages so it isn't an orphan. To keep a page out of search, put `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` above the markers.
- Never edit inside `<!-- seo:start ... seo:end -->` or the generated pages (each creator's `<creator>.html`, the trust pages `privacy`, `terms`, `refunds`, `security`, `contact`, `help` and `license`); change `data/` or the templates in `scripts/lib/` and rebuild. Product (`/shop/<id>`), release and event pages and the `/events` calendar are rendered by the server (`server/shop/`, `server/content/`), not files; news is offline until the community opens (#174).
- Private store and account pages (dashboard, settings, studio, cart, login, library, checkout, admin) carry `<meta name="robots" content="noindex">` and are listed in `robots.txt`, the `PRIVATE` patterns of `scripts/seo-test.js` and `.claude/skills/verify-site/check.js`, and get the strict CSP block in `_headers`. A new one goes in all four; a page that must only ever reach signed-in visitors also gets a gate (`functions/<page>.js` re-exporting `server/pages/guard.js`, plus its path in that file's `GATED`). Signed-in-only or signed-out-only links carry `data-auth="in"` (with `hidden`) or `data-auth="out"`; `js/account-nav.js` shows the right set. They load only same-site scripts (the CSP blocks the rest).
- `<main id="main">` is the skip-link target.
- Content comes from `data/` through scripts. Page markup holds structure and static copy only, and shouldn't gain inline `<style>`, `<script>` or `style=`.
- Basics: `lang`, viewport meta, one `h1`, no skipped heading levels, landmarks (`header`, `main`, `footer`), `alt` describing the image (not keywords; `alt=""` if decorative), `width`/`height` on images, descriptive link text.
